# Code of Conduct

Reels Blocker adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Gradrien). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads or block contributors who break these rules.
